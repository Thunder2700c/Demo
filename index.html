03 — Core Rule

Power ≠ Pull Value

A character achieving top-tier DPS does not automatically make them a mandatory pull. If your account already owns an equivalent on-field carry, pulling is luxury redundancy.

This archive exists to prevent waste. Every recommendation is filtered through realistic C0 baselines, team synergy, and long-term account health.
